Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are the admission requirements?

Admission requires a completed application form, previous academic records, and an interview with our admission committee. Specific requirements may vary by class level.

What is the fee structure?

Our fee structure varies by class and program. We offer flexible payment plans and scholarships for deserving students. Please contact our admission office for detailed fee information.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes, we offer both online and offline classes. Our online platform provides live interactive sessions with qualified teachers and access to digital learning resources.

What are the school timings?

Regular school hours are from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Evening classes for working students are available from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
